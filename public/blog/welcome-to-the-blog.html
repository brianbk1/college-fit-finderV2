Welcome to the College Fit Finder Blog

We built College Fit Finder around a simple idea: the "best" college isn't a ranking — it's the one that fits you. Your budget, your distance from home, the size of the classes you'll actually sit in, the weather you'll live in for four years, and yes, even whether there's a decent coffee shop near campus. This blog is where we'll dig into all of it.

What we'll write about

Roughly once a week, we'll publish something practical and honest about the college search. Expect posts on:

  • Admissions trends — what's actually changing, minus the panic.
  • Paying for college — financial aid, net price, and reading an offer letter without a decoder ring.
  • Campus life & fit — the intangibles that a ranking will never capture.
  • Using the tool well — tips for getting sharper matches out of your search.
💡 New here? Start with our College Guides for the fundamentals, then come back to the blog for what's timely.

Why a blog, and why weekly

The college landscape shifts constantly — deadlines, aid rules, testing policies, and the plain reality of what campuses are like. A guide can lay out the fundamentals, but a blog can keep pace with what's happening right now. Weekly is our promise to stay current without burying you in noise.

Thanks for reading. We're glad you're here — and we hope the tool helps you find a place that feels like the right fit.
